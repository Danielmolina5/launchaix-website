FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does LaunchAI do?

LaunchAI is a Miami marketing agency for real estate agents and small businesses. We provide daily real estate seller leads on WhatsApp, done-for-you testimonial and promotional videos, SEO and online reputation management, social media growth, and professional websites with lead capture and appointment booking.

How much does marketing cost with LaunchAI?

Daily Real Estate Leads are $49.99/month with a 7-day free trial. Testimonial videos start at $59.99, SEO & Online Reputation is $349.99/month, and professional websites start at $599.99. Complete growth strategies are custom quoted.

Do you work with realtors outside Miami?

Yes. We are based in South Florida and work with realtors, teams, brokerages and small businesses across the United States. Daily lead reports can cover up to 3 ZIP codes in your market.

How do the daily real estate leads work?

Every morning you receive a PDF on WhatsApp with expired listings and owner contact information, sellers with recent price cuts, and market stats for your ZIP codes, plus ready-to-send scripts in English and Spanish. Lead volume varies with market activity.

Do you buy or write reviews?

No. Our reputation service helps you invite real customers to share honest reviews on Google and Trustpilot. We never buy, write or filter reviews.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime from your LaunchAI account, and cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period.
